Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

This policy explains what the app example app stores about you and how it is used.

What we store

  • Your email address, used to identify your account and to contact you about it.
  • A hash of your password. We never store the password itself and cannot read it.
  • Your notes, exactly as you write them.
  • Photos you upload, stored on the server and shown only to you.
  • A session cookie, which keeps you signed in. It is needed for the app to work and is not used for tracking.

Who receives your data

No third parties receive your data. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services, and we do not sell or share your information.

Deleting your data

You can delete notes and photos in the app. To delete your whole account, contact us through the support page.
