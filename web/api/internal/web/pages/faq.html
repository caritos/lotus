Frequently asked questions

What is goweb?

goweb is a simple notes app. You sign in, write notes, and attach photos to them. It works in any modern web browser.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You sign up with an email address and a password so that only you can see your notes.

Where is my data stored?

Your notes, account details and uploaded photos are stored on the server that runs this site. They are not sent to any other company. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I delete my notes?

Yes. Deleting a note removes it from your list. If you want your whole account removed, contact us through the support page.
